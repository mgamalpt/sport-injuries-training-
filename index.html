  • • Eccentric hamstring, adductor & calf-Achilles load management

2. Sports Rehabilitation & RTS KPIs

Criteria-driven, milestone-based rehabilitation bridging early post-operative/acute protection with high-velocity field reconditioning and objective Return-to-Performance clearance.

  • • Post-op ACLR, lateral extra-articular tenodesis & meniscus rehab
  • • T-junction hamstring, groin & Achilles tendon reconditioning
  • • Blood Flow Restriction (BFR) & strength-speed profiling
  • • Shared decision-making with surgeons, physicians & S&C staff

3. Biomechanical Movement Analysis

Quantitative assessment of human locomotion, multi-directional deceleration, and sport-specific kinematics to eliminate compensatory movement strategies prior to RTS.

  • • 2D/3D kinematic & kinetic gait, sprint & cutting analysis
  • • Dual force-plate jump-landing, RFD & limb symmetry testing
  • • Surface EMG neuromuscular timing & activation diagnostics
  • • Isokinetic & handheld dynamometry torque-angle profiling
